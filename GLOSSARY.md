# Course Platform

An online course platform where learners buy courses and watch their lessons.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's request to get their money back on a course they bought, with their reason. It is pending until someone on the team approves or declines it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The amount the learner actually paid, sent back to the card they paid with once a Refund Request is approved. The learner loses access to the course.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The number of days after a purchase during which a learner can make a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the learner opens with their bank or card issuer, outside the platform. It is not a Refund Request.
